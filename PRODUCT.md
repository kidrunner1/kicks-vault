# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

KicksVault serves sneaker shoppers and collectors who want to browse curated products, inspect real size availability, add items to cart, and review orders without the interface feeling like a generic catalog.

## Product Purpose

KicksVault is a premium sneaker storefront with authentication, product management, size-based inventory, cart checkout, and order history. Success means the landing page builds desire and confidence, while the store pages make buying decisions clear from real product, price, and stock data.

## Brand Personality

Confident, cinematic, collectible. The brand should feel like a curated archive rather than a basic shop: focused on product presence, limited drops, clean movement, and practical buying clarity.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic e-commerce templates, decorative card grids that do not explain value, fake filters, stock imagery that hides the product, and UI that looks disconnected from real inventory or checkout behavior.

## Design Principles

- Show the product first: imagery and product data should carry the experience.
- Make commerce confidence visible: stock, checkout, auth, and order status should feel trustworthy.
- Keep landing sections purposeful: each section should either create desire or reduce buying friction.
- Use motion as pacing, not decoration.
- Preserve the premium monochrome base, then use restrained contrast and product imagery for energy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target accessible contrast for body text and controls. Motion should remain readable and must not hide content. Interactions should remain understandable without relying on hover alone.
